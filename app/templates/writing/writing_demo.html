Course Details

Course Detail Card Components

Business Writing

A course designed to improve effective persuasive communication skills for English business performance in a global corporate environment. By training essential business writing skills needed for work, you will improve your ability to effectively convey your opinions to others.

  • Biz Email: Complaint, Apology, Request, Sales, Announcement, Thanks mail pattern training
  • Biz Meeting: Preparation pattern training for meaningful conclusions
  • Biz Negotiation: Win-win strategy and planning pattern training
  • Biz Presentation: PT planning and PPT creation pattern training through information (charts, graphs)
Monthly
3 topics + 1 PBW

Practical Business Writing

A course that strengthens the ability to immediately apply to practical work by presenting various Business Forms currently used in English-speaking business sites and re-writing them to apply to learners' practical work.

  • Fields: Planning, Management, Legal, Human Resources, Finance, Accounting, Sales, Marketing, Operations, Logistics, IT, Consultants, Contractors, Credit & Collection, Real Estate
  • Lineup: Notices, Worksheets, Statements, Plans, Checklists, Certificates, Agreements, Emails, Letters, Scripts, Requests, Guidelines, Outlines, Proposals, Evaluations, Policies, Surveys
Monthly
1 field x 4 forms

General Writing

A training course for understanding proposed topics from the basic to advanced stages of English composition and writing essays with logical and systematic structures.

  • Beginner (L1-L3): Basic stage - Understanding paragraph composition elements, basic English composition using short sentences
  • Intermediate (L4-L6): Intermediate stage - Learning to write arguments according to introduction, body, conclusion format, acquiring the ability to write natural essays
  • Advanced (L7-L9): Advanced stage - Learning solid composition and logical development, acquiring the ability to write creative essays using examples, reasons, and details
