Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our programs

Phone English

How long are the phone lessons?
We offer flexible session lengths: 10, 15, 20, and 30 minute options. You can choose the duration that best fits your schedule and learning goals. Most students start with 15-minute sessions and gradually increase as they build confidence.
Can I choose my instructor?
Yes! You can browse our instructor profiles and select the ones that match your preferences. We have 500+ certified native speakers from the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.
What if I miss a scheduled lesson?
You can cancel or reschedule up to 2 hours before your lesson without penalty. If you miss a lesson without notice, it will be counted as completed.

Corporate Training

Is on-site training available?
Yes, we offer on-site training at your office location. Our instructors can come to your workplace for group sessions or individual coaching.
How do you track employee progress?
We provide detailed monthly reports including attendance, skill assessments, and progress metrics. HR managers get access to a dedicated dashboard for real-time tracking.
